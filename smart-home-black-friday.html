Smart Home Black Friday 2026: What to Buy and What to Skip

By SmartHomeTechHQ

Black Friday is the best week of the year to buy smart home gear — and the easiest week to waste money on it. The deals are real: the big brands discount their best devices every year, and starter kits that are overpriced in October become genuinely good value in late November. The trick is knowing what to buy before the sale starts, so you are choosing from a list instead of reacting to a banner.

Buy the boring essentials first. Smart plugs, smart bulbs and video doorbells are the categories with the deepest and most reliable discounts, and they are also the devices you will actually use every day. A four-pack of smart plugs or a bundle of bulbs at a sale price is a better buy than any single flashy gadget, because it upgrades a whole room at once. Thermostats and robot vacuums also drop sharply, but only buy one if you have already checked that it works with your platform — a cheap thermostat that does not talk to your hub is not a bargain at any price. Our best smart plugs for beginners guide shows which plugs are worth owning before you look at a single price tag.

Skip anything that needs a subscription to do its basic job. A doorbell that charges monthly just to keep your video clips, or a camera that locks person detection behind a paywall, will cost you far more over two years than the discount saved you on day one. Skip no-name brands with no track record, even at half price — if the company disappears, the app stops working and the device becomes a paperweight. And skip buying a second hub: if you already have Alexa, Google Home or HomeKit, buy devices that work with it instead of starting a whole new system.

Make your list now, while prices are still normal. Write down the three devices your home actually needs, check their usual prices, and set a simple rule: buy only if the sale price beats the usual price by a clear margin, and only if the device works with your setup. Then check your starter kit plans against our best smart home starter kits compared guide so the sale buys fit the home you are building, not just the cart you are filling. The best Black Friday deal is the one you planned for — everything else is just noise.
